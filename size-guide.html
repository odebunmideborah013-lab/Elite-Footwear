FIND YOUR FIT

At our atelier, every pair of elite footwear is handcrafted to exceptional standards. To ensure you experience the absolute comfort and striking silhouette intended by our artisans, finding your precise size is essential.

How to Measure Your Feet

For the most accurate fit, we recommend measuring your feet at the end of the day when they are at their largest:

  1. Set Up: Place a blank sheet of paper flat on the floor, aligned flush against a wall.
  2. Step On: Stand naturally on the paper with your heel lightly touching the wall.
  3. Mark: Using a pencil held completely upright, mark the tip of your longest toe on the paper.
  4. Measure: Use a ruler to measure the exact distance from the edge of the paper to your mark in centimeters (cm).

Check Both: Repeat this process for your other foot. If one foot is slightly larger, always use the larger measurement.
